Easemap privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Easemap is a personal pain diary. This page says exactly what happens to your information.

We collect no data

Easemap does not collect, receive, store or share any of your information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking. The app only loads its own files (including the 3D body models) from the address it is served from and never sends your information to any server; it uses no third-party fonts, scripts or services.

Everything stays on your device

What you enter (body locations, pain ratings, times, notes, medications, triggers, your name and date of birth if you choose to add them) and your settings are stored only on your device, in your browser or app storage (IndexedDB and localStorage). Easemap's makers and servers never receive it; anyone who can unlock your device can open it.

Because the data lives only on your device, it can be lost if you clear website data, delete the app or lose the device. Easemap offers a backup export so you can keep your own copy.

Clinical edition

The clinical edition of Easemap is for clinics and hospital units. The clinic or hospital is the custodian of any patient data entered on its devices; the makers of Easemap never receive it. Patient records are encrypted on the device with a staff passphrase and lock when the device is idle. The in-clinic quick session keeps nothing after it ends.

Exports and sharing are your choice

Your information leaves your device only when you ask it to:

  • Backups (a file you save yourself, optionally encrypted with a passphrase only you know).
  • Reports that you print, save or share.
  • Doctor links and QR codes: the information is encrypted and placed in the link itself, after the # sign, which browsers do not send to web servers. Anyone you give the link to (and the passcode, if you set one) can open it.
  • Calendar reminders (an .ics file you add to your own calendar).

Where those files go after you save or share them is up to you and the app you choose, and is covered by that app's own policy.

If Easemap is inside another app

Easemap can be shown inside another app (a host app). Only if you turn on the setting that asks whether to share a summary with that app does Easemap pass it a summary of entries (areas, intensity, times, medications and triggers). Notes, your name, your date of birth and exact pin positions are never included. You can turn this off at any time. What the host app does with a summary is covered by that app's privacy policy.

Purchases

If you buy a subscription or licence, the app store or payment processor handles your purchase and payment details. No health data is involved in a purchase, and your pain log is never sent to the store, the payment processor or to us. The licence key you receive is checked on your device.

Children

Easemap is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Medical disclaimer

Easemap is a patient-reported log. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice.

Easemap is not an emergency service. If you have sudden, severe or chest pain, or think it is an emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Questions: contact the support address listed on the app's store page.
