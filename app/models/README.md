# public/models

Real human anatomy for Easemap, generated by `npm run anatomy` (see `scripts/anatomy/`); do not edit by hand.

Male body, model id **`bp3d-male-apose-8`** (supersedes `bp3d-male-apose-1` to `-7`; apose-8 is apose-7 with the knee source-mirror label, geometry byte-identical, see the last section; the Phase 7 and Phase A sections at the end of this file say what changed; the skin is byte-identical to apose-6). Female body, model id **`hra-female-apose-6`** (supersedes `hra-female-apose-1` to `-5`).
**These models are not clinically reviewed**; the review gate is `phase5/ANATOMY-REVIEW-CHECKLIST.md`. The personal-app TestFlight build stays on the frozen `bp3d-male-apose-6` / `hra-female-apose-5` pair until the owner accepts the Phase A rebuild.

**Size rule (owner decision D1, one rule for both sexes; the numbers live in `scripts/anatomy/sizeRule.mjs`, which `build.mjs`, `buildFemale.mjs` and `quality.mjs` import, so they are not repeated in code):** `body.glb` is at most 7.6 MiB, `body.glb` plus `body-sensitive.glb` at most 8 MiB, and `body.glb` at most 445,000 triangles (the existing quality gate, unchanged). These are size limits, not accuracy gates. Nothing raises the cap during a build: an over-cap build reduces or drops content like any other. Triangles of `body.glb` plus the sensitive file are reported (provenance `sizes.combinedTriangles`), not gated.

**Only two model folders ship:** `bp3d-male-apose-6/` and `hra-female-apose-5/`. The folders of superseded models (`bp3d-male-apose-2` to `-5`, `hra-female-apose-1` to `-4`) were removed from the working tree to save space (they remain in git history); their ids stay in `src/body/modelCatalog.ts` (`supersedes`) so older pins, backups and doctor links still migrate. Later sections of this file describe those earlier builds as history; their files are no longer on disk.
The current female model is `hra-female-apose-5/` (see "Female model" below).
Every mesh carries a `NORMAL` attribute (normalised BYTE, stride 4, `KHR_mesh_quantization`, listed in
`extensionsRequired`) and `COLOR_0` (normalised UNSIGNED_BYTE, stride 4) holding baked ambient occlusion
(`0.55 + 0.45 * visibility`, grey). Skin normals are computed once on the welded whole skin, so coincident
border vertices of neighbouring regions have identical normals and there is no shading seam at region borders.

`body.glb` contains **no** sensitive mesh (the build fails otherwise and the report lists any it finds).
An App Store build can drop `body-sensitive.glb` (`VITE_SENSITIVE_ASSETS=0`) without a code change.
Current numbers are in `artifacts/anatomy-report.json` (region counts, triangles, sizes, pose metrics).

### Model identity

The root node of each GLB carries `extras: { modelId, sex, pose, supersedes, layers, attribution, attributions, provenance, clinicalReview }` (for example
`bp3d-male-apose-4`, `male`, `apose`, `['bp3d-male-apose-1','bp3d-male-apose-2','bp3d-male-apose-3']`, `['skin','muscle','skeleton','organs']`; `provenance` is the path of the
provenance JSON and `clinicalReview` is `'pending (phase5/ANATOMY-REVIEW-CHECKLIST.md)'`). Every region node carries `extras: { regionId, layer, sensitive }`, plus
`approximate: true` and `fitMethod` (`scaled`, `hra-similarity-fit`, `parametric-fallback`, `parametric`, `procedural-tube` or `level-set-carve`) where the part is built by
approximation or adaptation (the male coccyx is `parametric-fallback`; joint bands are `proximity-carve`; female warped parts are `bp3d-male-tps`). Node names are the region id with `.` replaced by `__`. Pins are stored against `modelId`; the id is
bumped only when surfaces move by more than about 3 mm (re-pose, re-segmentation).

### Modifications (made by this project)

1. **Converted** from binary STL to glTF 2.0 binary with positions, int8 normals and baked ambient occlusion colours.
2. **Transformed**: vertices welded (0.01 mm grid); rotated from the source frame (mm, Z up,
   anterior -Y) into the app frame (metres, Y up, patient-left +X, anterior +Z) and recentred on the skin.
3. **Segmented**: the source skin is a single mesh mixing the outer surface with nested inner
   shells; only the outer surface is kept (visibility test from many directions, run on the A-posed body so
   the axilla, crotch and inner thighs are included) and it is divided into the app's skin regions by nearest
   bone region plus joint bands and body landmarks. The pelvic floor and genital area (perineum, anal area,
   groin, buttock, penis shaft, glans, scrotum) are segmented by anatomical anchors: the organ and muscle
   meshes (glans, corpora, testes, external anal sphincter, gluteus maximus) and landmarks of the hip bones
   (ischial tuberosity, pubic arch, ASIS, pubic tubercle).
   Muscles, bones and organs are mapped from FMA part names to the app's regions (`scripts/anatomy/rules.mjs`);
   unlisted parts (deep muscles, ligaments, teeth, pancreas, ...) are omitted. Left/right follow the
   part name (or the sign of x for unsided parts).
4. **Re-posed** to an A-pose at build time (`scripts/anatomy/pose.mjs`): each limb is rotated about its
   humeral or femoral head centre with per-vertex angle-blended weights (humerus axis to 40 degrees from
   vertical, femoral axes to 10 degrees each); muscles follow the nearest skin vertex, long bones rotate
   rigidly, trunk, pelvis and organs stay. A metrics gate (triangle stretch, muscle-inside-skin share,
   hand-to-thigh clearance) is recorded in the report.
5. **Repaired the whole posed skin once** before it is split into regions (`scripts/anatomy/skinLib.mjs`,
   `cleanupSkin` in `build.mjs`; PLAN4 section 1B): triangles stretched more than 2.5x by the pose inside
   the crotch box and the two axilla spheres were cut out; every hole except small head orifices
   (nostrils, ears) was filled (Liepa dynamic-programming triangulation, recursive splitting of big loops,
   refinement, fairing); the skin was isotropically remeshed (Botsch-Kobbelt, label borders preserved),
   smoothed inside the repair boxes (Taubin, crease relaxation), region borders were smoothed, and the surface
   was re-meshed once more at per-region densities taken from a meshoptimizer run (curvature adaptive, with
   floors for the hands, head and pelvic regions), flipped for minimum angle, and nudged to remove needle
   triangles. Hidden crevices that the visibility test cannot see are therefore filled with smooth membranes
   (for example the armpit pit and the web between the fingers), not scan geometry. The ragged cut edges of the
   armpit holes are faired (curve Taubin smoothing of the loop plus its neighbouring rings) before they are filled,
   and the armpit webs get extra fairing on the coarse mesh, so the web reads as a clean fold. Connected skin
   fragments below 500 triangles (scan flaps above the shoulders, interior finger sheets, eyelid scraps) are
   dropped at the end; the quality gate requires every skin component to have at least 500 triangles.
6. **Generated** a genital proxy surface (`skin.genitals.c`). First the outline of the sensitive patch is reshaped
   (`reshapePatchFootprint` in `proxy.mjs`): the raised core (shaft, glans, scrotal bulge) is found against a membrane
   over the raw patch, grown by 10 mm along the surface, closed and opened geodesically so notches and spikes of the
   outline disappear, and extended over the narrow slot between scrotum and perineum; flanges of flat groin skin leave
   the patch, so the patch (and with it the proxy rim) is one smooth, rounded outline on flat skin. The patch is replaced
   by a membrane over its single boundary loop plus a gentle dome (10 mm, smootherstep of the geodesic distance to the
   rim, so it leaves the surrounding skin tangentially), isotropically remeshed with every rim vertex frozen, smoothed
   together with its neighbours. It has no anatomical detail and shares its boundary vertices (and their normals, taken
   from the surface the default view shows) with the surrounding skin so it closes the hole left when
   `body-sensitive.glb` is not loaded.
   The proxy and a band of neighbouring skin (7 rings) are then relaxed together with a shrinking Laplacian (the rim loop with a curve
   Taubin pass), which draws the mound back onto the lower-abdomen and inner-thigh surface: the silhouette from the front and side is a
   gentle convex curve with no pendant shaft or lower lip. Before that the sensitive patch and a 5-ring band of skin are Taubin-blended,
   the real patch follows the moved rim (displacement carried over 20 mm), and normals of the rim (smoothed along the loop) and of a few
   rings on both sides are feathered so shading crosses the junction without a collar. The sensitive patch itself is remeshed finer (glans about 1.6 mm, shaft about
   2.2 mm) and smoothed.
   WP1d added three steps to this polish (`fillGroove`, `blendPatchToProxy`, `polishJunction` in `proxy.mjs`). (a) Default view: the
   narrow slot behind the scrotum, where the real surface drops between the proxy and a fin of perineum skin (about 18 mm deep and
   9 mm wide at the midline), is filled: the heights of the vertices around it are replaced, with a smootherstep falloff, by a Gaussian mean of the
   heights of their neighbours in the horizontal plane (tangential smoothing cannot close a pocket whose walls are vertical), followed by Laplacian
   fairing, so the surface runs from the perineum into the mound without a notch (the fin lip comes down about 7 mm, the pocket floor
   rises about 18 mm; all inside the crotch box). The patch rim follows through the existing rim carry. (b) Sensitive view: every patch vertex
   within 12 mm (geodesic) of the rim has its height over the proxy surface scaled by a smootherstep of that distance, so the real anatomy
   leaves the skin tangentially like the proxy does; then the rim loop (curve Taubin), 8 rings of the patch, 7 rings of the neighbouring skin and 8 rings of the
   proxy are smoothed together (Taubin, weights falling off with the ring), so there is no ledge or serrated flange along the rim; the
   proxy interior is evened out afterwards (Taubin with the rim fixed, edge flips for the minimum angle). (c) Shading: rim normals follow the default view
   except where the two configurations leave the rim at more than 30 degrees (the lower flanks where the real scrotum meets the thigh in a
   crease): there they lean up to half toward the sensitive configuration (weight smootherstep over 30..60 degrees); baked ambient occlusion
   of the patch is blended from the default-view value at the rim to its own value (baked with the patch present, so the scrotal base and
   shaft root keep their creases) over 5 rings.
7. **Decimated** the other layers with meshoptimizer (budgets: skin 100k, muscle 156k, skeleton 108k,
   organs 60k triangles; 420k max; both GLBs together at most 8 MB). Per-vertex ambient occlusion
   (64 directions, 512 x 512 depth maps, per layer) is baked into `COLOR_0`.

### Phase 5 male model (`bp3d-male-apose-3`)

The skin, muscles, bones and organs of apose-2 are rebuilt by the same pipeline and the non-carved skin is identical to it (checked
against a baseline build, positions and indices equal). Added or changed, all schematic and **not clinically reviewed**:

1. **Urethra regenerated** as a swept tube in five named segments (prostate part, pelvic floor part, bulb part behind the scrotum, penile
   shaft part, tip part inside the glans) from the prostate through the spongiosum to the opening. It replaces the single source urethra
   (`organs.urethra.c`, retired). Approximate (`procedural-tube`).
2. **Sub-regions defined** by geometric cuts of the glans, shaft and scrotum skin (`carveMale.mjs`, original vertices never move): the
   rim of the glans (corona), the frenulum, the urethral opening (meatus), the shaft in three bands (base, middle, near the glans) and the
   scrotum in left and right halves (`skin.scrotum.c` is retired). Approximate (`level-set-carve`) except the glans remainder.
3. **Testes enlarged** toward an adult reference volume (limited by the scrotal skin; measured volumes are in the provenance and the
   checklist) and the **epididymis** is now its own part. Approximate (`scaled`).
4. **Coccyx added** as a schematic chain of four ellipsoids (parametric shape by this project, `parametric-fallback`): the fit of the Human Reference Atlas
   coccyx to the BodyParts3D sacrum failed its checks, so no Human Reference Atlas geometry ships in the male files. Approximate.
5. **Perineal body added** as a schematic wedge (flattened ellipsoid, long axis transverse, top tilted back) between the bulb and the anal area,
   shrunk until it lies inside the skin. Approximate (`parametric`).
6. **Deep muscles added** from BodyParts3D parts: pelvic floor (now left and right; `muscle.pelvic_floor.c` is retired), external anal
   sphincter (no longer part of the rectum), obturator internus, piriformis, iliopsoas (psoas major and iliacus) and quadratus lumborum.
   The pelvic floor, sphincter, obturator internus and piriformis stay at rest like the pelvis; iliopsoas and quadratus lumborum follow the skin.

Approximate in this model (extras `approximate: true`, shown with the approximate flag in the app): `organs.testis.l/.r`, `organs.epididymis.l/.r`,
`skeleton.coccyx.c`, `muscle.perineal_body.c`, the five `organs.urethra_*` segments, and the carved skin `skin.urethral_meatus.c`,
`skin.corona.c`, `skin.frenulum.c`, `skin.penis_base.c`, `skin.penis_shaft.c`, `skin.penis_distal.c`, `skin.scrotum.l`, `skin.scrotum.r`.
The list is mirrored in `src/body/modelCatalog.ts` and checked against the files by a unit test.

Budgets: skin 100k, muscle 156k, skeleton 108k, organs 60k triangles (420k total, both files at most 8 MB). Quality gates add the
sub-region, urethra, coccyx and perineal-body gates, a skin regression against the baseline and a check that existing muscles and
organs keep at least 85% of their triangles. Every generated or fitted asset is logged in
`scripts/anatomy/provenance/bp3d-male-apose-3.json` (sources and hashes, parameters, metrics, `reviewStatus: 'not clinically reviewed'`; apose-4 has its own file, described below).
`node scripts/anatomy/reviewChecklist.mjs` writes `phase5/ANATOMY-REVIEW-CHECKLIST.md` for the clinician review; passing the build
gates measures geometry only and is not a clinical review.

### Male model apose-4 (skin labels and anal-zone shading)

are the same as in apose-3 (227 nodes in both files). **Not clinically reviewed.**

What changed:

1. **Suprapubic midline.** The groin regions used to run to the midline above the penis, so a tap there was titled "Left groin" or "Right groin". Now the groin
   stops at the pubic tubercle (less 5 mm): `skin.groin.l` starts 43.3 mm left of the midline, `skin.groin.r` 43.4 mm right
   of it (area ratio 1.002), and the strip between them is `skin.pelvis.c`.
2. **Natal cleft.** The piece of `skin.pelvis.c` behind the body (the cleft, 244 triangles) is split along the midline into `skin.buttock.l` and `skin.buttock.r`
   (as in the female model); `skin.pelvis.c` is one anterior piece (no triangle with its centroid at z <= 0).
3. **Knee strip.** A strip of the thigh that had been given to the knee is back in the thigh: both knee regions now end at the same height (tops differ by 1.0 mm).
4. **Islands.** Small label islands in the armpit web (chest and upper back next to the arm) go to the arm; every skin region is one connected piece except
   `skin.thigh.l`, which keeps a thin closed fold of 37 triangles that touches the rest of the skin only through the knee region (thigh skin, kept as thigh so
   the knee ends at the knee). The internal labels of the opt-in patch are not changed.
5. **Anal-zone shading.** The baked occlusion around the anus and along the perineal raphe showed dark speckles (the curvature term of the refined zone is noisy).
   The cavity term is now smoothed (3 passes) and limited to the dimple, its verge and the raphe. Speckle (standard deviation of the occlusion minus the mean of its
   neighbours, vertices within 15 mm of the anal anchor or 8 mm of the raphe, outside the 6 mm dimple core): before 0.03613 with 14 outlier vertices, after 0.01044 with
   0 (limits 0.02 and 0). The dimple is still the darkest part of the zone (visibility gate unchanged).

**Method (hybrid).** Built by `npm run anatomy:female` (`scripts/anatomy/buildFemale.mjs`; helpers `pelvicLandmarks.mjs`, `femaleDeep.mjs`,
`femaleStages.mjs`, `isoCarve.mjs`). Deterministic: no randomness, a cache-only rebuild is byte-identical.

1. **Real Human Reference Atlas parts (not approximate).** Skin (v1.3) and organs (v1.2) as before. New: the pelvis, sacrum and coccyx
   (`VH_Female/v1.2/VH_F_Pelvis.glb`). The compact-bone shells of the pubis, ilium and ischium and the sacrum are merged into `skeleton.sacrum_pelvis.c`
   (simplified to 10,000 triangles); the coccyx is `skeleton.coccyx.c` (700 triangles). The spongy-bone interiors are left out (invisible, they would

2. **Adapted from the male model (approximate, `fitMethod` `bp3d-male-tps`).** Every other bone and every muscle, including the deep muscles and the
   left and right pelvic floor of `bp3d-male-apose-4` (BodyParts3D; `bp3d-male-apose-3` for apose-2), is warped into the female body with a thin-plate spline on 31 skin landmarks plus
   9 pelvic bony landmarks (symphysis, ASIS, iliac crest, ischial tuberosity, sacral apex; found with the same code on both pelves). The long bones
   (femur, patella, tibia/fibula, humerus, radius/ulna, clavicle) are then refitted rigid plus scale to their warped positions so they do not shear
   (`bp3d-male-tps+procrustes`). The lumbar spine is moved a few millimetres onto the HRA sacrum and each femur is seated in its hip socket.
   Whatever still lies outside the female skin is brought inside: bones are shrunk about their centre when more than 3% of a bone is outside (never
   below 88%; skull, hands and some limb bones) and pushed 1.5 mm inside; muscles are pushed 2 mm inside. The measured shares before and after are
   in the provenance and the checklist.
3. **Pelvic floor.** The male levator ani has no opening wide enough for the vagina and rectum, so a hole is carved around them (iso-surface boolean on a
   2 mm grid with 3 mm clearance, cutter mirrored about the midline so left and right stay balanced) and the result is simplified. Its shape is male-derived:
   the checklist asks the reviewer to check it for pelvic-floor physiotherapy use.
4. **Perineal body.** Parametric: the same helper as the male (`pelvicPrimitives.perinealBody`), placed between the back edge of the vaginal opening
   and the anal anchor, kept inside the skin and clear of the vagina and rectum (`fitMethod` `parametric`). Approximate.
5. **Stomach and oesophagus.** The Human Reference Atlas female set at the pinned commit has no stomach or oesophagus file (14 candidate paths were
   probed on raw.githubusercontent.com; every one is a 404, recorded under `absent` in `scripts/anatomy/hra.lock.json`). They are warped from the male model,
   moved clear of the liver and spleen and kept inside the skin. Approximate (`bp3d-male-tps`).

### Known limitations

- The source model is a single adult male and BodyParts3D v3.0 has no female pelvic organs. The female model is a separate build
  from the Human Reference Atlas (`hra-female-apose-2`); its pelvis, sacrum and coccyx are Human Reference Atlas bone, its other muscles and bones
  are adapted from the male model by a landmark warp (approximate), and its stomach and oesophagus are warped from the male model too.
- The female pelvic floor is the male-derived levator with a hole cut around the vagina and rectum; the female hands, skull and some limb bones are
  shrunk a little (never below 88%) to sit inside the skin. Both are labelled approximate and listed in the review checklist.
- Shaft top and underside are not separate regions, prostate zones are not modelled, and the testes are scaled by a limited factor.
- The skin regions are anatomically sensible but approximate (joint bands, landmark heights, jagged borders
  at the resolution of the source triangles).
- Where the source skin is fused (arms touching the trunk) the A-pose stretches the fused triangles into a web at the
  armpits (about 0.4% of triangles stretch more than 2.5x; the gate allows 0.5%). The visibility cull is run on the posed
  body, so the inner thighs and the crotch exist, but the A-pose also opens a gap of up to about 1.7 cm between the scrotum's
  sides and the inner thighs where the source skin has no surface; the genital proxy and the sensitive skin do not cover it.
- The genital proxy is a smoothed membrane mound over the sensitive patch, not a model of anatomy, and it has about one
  triangle per vertex of its boundary (about 500 triangles, 131 boundary vertices). Its lower end is a narrow tongue between the two
  inner-thigh creases that ends in a soft arch where it meets the perineum (the pocket in front of the perineal fin is filled, but
  the V between the thighs is real geometry of the A-posed source, so a faint tongue and a shallow dent beside the crotch pit remain
  visible from below). With the 18+ gate on, the patch rim is smooth on both flanks; a slight lighter strip can remain along the lower left
  flank, where the real scrotum meets the thigh at a crease that the default view bridges (the rim normals there are a compromise between
  the two configurations, because the files must agree on shared normals).
- `organs.heart.c` comes from the whole-heart wall mesh (`FMA7274`), which carries internal structure.
- Peripheral nerves are not in BodyParts3D v3.0, so there is no nerves layer. Knee ligaments and menisci, bursae, vessels and the small joints are not modelled either.
