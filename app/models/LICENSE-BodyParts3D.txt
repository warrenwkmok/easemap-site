Changes made (CC BY-SA requires indicating them)
------------------------------------------------
Source: BodyParts3D v3.0 (20110915), as converted from OBJ to binary STL and mirrored at
https://github.com/Kevin-Mattheus-Moerman/BodyParts3D (commit f0eeb6e843380cfe6b83797cf8c3e1af74de5e61).
Our changes to that data:
  * converted: STL -> glTF 2.0 binary (GLB), positions only, vertex normals computed at load;
  * transformed: vertices welded, rotated into a Y-up, metre-scale frame and recentred on the skin;
  * segmented: the skin surface was reduced to its outer shell (internal shells removed) and divided
    into named body regions (head, neck, chest, ... and, by anatomical anchors, the perineum, anal area,
    groin, buttocks, penis shaft, glans and scrotum); individual muscle, bone and organ parts were merged
    into region meshes; many parts (deep muscles, ligaments, teeth, pancreas, ...) were omitted;
  * re-posed: the whole body was rotated from the source standing pose into an A-pose (arms and legs
    rotated about the shoulder and hip joints, with blended skin weights);
  * generated: a smoothed genital proxy surface (no anatomical detail) from the source skin patch;
  * split: sensitive regions were written to a separate file, body-sensitive.glb;
  * decimated: meshes were simplified (meshoptimizer) to about 415,000 triangles in total.
The upstream converter's own changes (OBJ -> STL, file selection) are described in the upstream
README at the commit above.
