The model files are modified adaptations. They are distributed as a whole under CC BY-SA 2.1 JP (see the notes below).

Male body

BodyParts3D © The Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS), licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 2.1 Japan (CC BY-SA 2.1 JP). Modified: segmented, re-posed, remeshed, urethra regenerated, sub-regions defined, schematic surface relief, coccyx and perineal body added (schematic shapes by this project).

The coccyx and the perineal body of the male body are schematic shapes made by this project (approximate); no Human Reference Atlas data is in the male files. Joint regions are bands of the bone surfaces near the neighbouring bone, defined by this project (approximate). Further muscles, tendons, ligaments, discs and the pancreas are BodyParts3D parts (CC BY-SA 2.1 JP).

Female body

Human Reference Atlas 3D Reference Object Library, HuBMAP / NIH Common Fund, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0) (ccf-3d-reference-object-library @ f1a3a63), derived from the NLM Visible Human Project. Modified: segmented, simplified; nipple and areola removed; schematic external genital detail (including the anal dimple) and urethra/clitoris shapes by this project; pancreas, round and uterosacral ligaments are HRA files; muscles, discs, joints and bones except pelvis and coccyx adapted from BodyParts3D (male) by a landmark warp: approximate. Model files: CC BY-SA 2.1 JP.

Muscles, bones except pelvis and coccyx, stomach and oesophagus: BodyParts3D © DBCLS, CC BY-SA 2.1 JP, adapted to the female body by a landmark warp (approximate); BodyParts3D is also the label source for the skin regions (credited above). The discs, the ligaments of the bones, the new muscles and the joint regions (bands of the bone surfaces near the neighbouring bone, defined by this project) are adapted from BodyParts3D in the same way and are approximate.

Anatomy names and plain-language terms

The names the app shows for body areas and structures come from a term table built from the sources below. The anatomy names have not been checked by a clinician. A name that no cited source supports is not shown as fact.

Foundational Model of Anatomy (FMA)

Standard anatomy names and identifiers. Structural Informatics Group, University of Washington, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY). Read through the copy distributed with BodyParts3D; an extract of the classes used is stored with the app's build tools. Names are looked up, shortened or merged for display. No endorsement is implied.
