Easemap support

Easemap is a private body pain log. It stores everything on your phone and has no account.

Contact

Email hi@heysusan.app. Please do not send personal health details by email.

Common questions

Can you recover my entries if I lose my phone? No. Nothing is sent to us, so there is nothing to recover. Use the backup and export options in the app.

Is the anatomy information medical advice? No. Easemap is not a medical device and its terms are not checked by a clinician. Talk to a doctor about any pain.

How do I delete my data? Delete the app, or use the delete option in the app settings.
