What Easemap does

Easemap is a personal pain diary. You tap a 3D body to show where it hurts, from the skin down to muscle, bone and organs, and rate the pain. Easemap shows the everyday and medical names for that spot and can make a report or a body-chart image for you to bring to an appointment.

What we collect

Nothing. The makers of Easemap do not collect, receive, store, sell or share your personal information or your health information.

  • There is no account and no sign-in.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking.
  • The app does not connect to our servers or anyone else's. It uses no third-party scripts, fonts or services. The 3D body models and everything else it needs are inside the app.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter is stored only on your phone or tablet, in the app's own local database and settings storage. That includes:

  • where it hurts (body location and layer), pain ratings, dates and times;
  • notes, how the pain feels, medications and possible triggers you type in;
  • your name and date of birth, only if you choose to add them for your reports;
  • your settings, such as which body you chose.

The makers of Easemap never receive this information. Anyone who can unlock your device can open the app and see it, so keep your device locked. Easemap relies on your device's own protection (passcode, Face ID or Touch ID, and the device's encryption); it does not add its own passcode to the diary.

If you delete the app, your diary is deleted with it, unless you saved a backup first. If your device makes its own backup (for example iCloud Backup or a computer backup), that backup may include the app's storage. That is controlled by your device settings and Apple's terms, not by Easemap.

When something leaves your device

Only when you tap to save or share it. Easemap then hands the file to the app you pick (for example Files, Mail or Messages) through your device's share sheet. What happens after that is covered by that app's own policy. Things you can save or share:

  • A report (print or PDF) with what you logged, body diagrams and, if you added them, your name and date of birth.
  • A body-chart image that shows where it hurts and how much. No name, dates, notes or medications are drawn on it, and hidden image data (metadata) is removed. Look at it before you send it.
  • A backup file (see below).
  • A calendar reminder file that you can add to your own calendar.

Some of these features may not be available in every version of the app.

Backups

A backup is a file you create and keep yourself. Easemap does not upload it anywhere.

  • Encrypted backup (recommended). You choose a passphrase of at least 12 characters. Easemap encrypts the backup on your device with AES-256-GCM, using a key made from your passphrase with PBKDF2-SHA256 (600,000 rounds). Without the passphrase the backup cannot be opened, and we cannot recover a lost passphrase for you.
  • Unencrypted backup. An advanced option saves a plain, readable file. Anyone who gets that file can read it.

You can restore a backup with Import backup in Settings.

Doctor links

Where your version offers it, you can make a link or QR code that lets your doctor open a read-only copy of your report. The report is encrypted on your device and placed in the link itself, after the # sign. Web browsers do not send that part of a link to web servers, so the makers of Easemap do not receive your report. The key is either in the link or protected by a passcode you share separately. Anyone who has the link (and the passcode, if you set one) can open it, and a link cannot be cancelled after you share it, so share it only with people you trust.

Your choices and rights

  • See your data: everything is in the app. You can export a copy at any time.
  • Correct it: edit or delete any entry in the app.
  • Delete it: Settings, "Delete all data and settings", or delete the app.
  • Take it with you: export a backup file.

Because we do not hold any of your information, we cannot look up, change or delete it for you: you are in control of it on your device. Privacy laws where you live (for example in Canada, the European Union or California) may give you rights over personal information that a company holds; Easemap's makers hold none. You can still contact us with any privacy question.

Children

Easemap is not directed to children under 13, and it collects no information from anyone. Detailed genital and urinary anatomy is hidden by default and is shown only after a person confirms they are 18 or older.

Not a medical device

Easemap is a patient-reported log. It is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice, and the names it shows describe anatomy at a location, not what is wrong. Talk to your doctor about your pain.

If you have sudden, severe or chest pain, or think it is an emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number.

Purchases

This version has no purchases. If paid features are added, purchases will be handled by Apple through the App Store. No health information is part of a purchase, and your diary is not sent to Apple or to us. This section will be updated before that happens.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date at the top. If a change would mean any of your information leaves your device in a new way, we will say so in the app before it happens, and the change will apply only after you agree.
